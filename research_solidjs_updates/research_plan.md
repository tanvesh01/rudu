# SolidJS updates research plan

## Main question
What are the newest meaningful SolidJS updates, and what direction is the project taking as of August 15, 2026?

## Research tracks
1. **Core framework and official releases** — recent SolidJS releases, important runtime/API changes, and current roadmap signals from official repositories, release notes, and project posts.
2. **SolidStart and ecosystem** — recent SolidStart, router, server/runtime, and tooling changes from official documentation and repositories.
3. **Community context and adoption** — notable announcements, project activity, and developer-facing implications, clearly separating sourced facts from interpretation.

## Synthesis
Combine the tracks into a concise timeline and practical summary: what shipped, what is changing, what developers should care about now, and what remains experimental or uncertain. Prefer primary sources and include dates and URLs.
